BC-250 Power Board

Perfboard map v12, drawn on your real board: a 70×90 perfboard with 24 columns × 30 rows, with the Pico and ATX header where you placed them. Column 24 is on the right (ATX side), and row 1 sits against the honeycomb wall. The Pico is in columns 6 and 13, rows 5–24. The ATX latch faces the wall, so only two ATX wires come from the far row.

The coloured lines are solder paths on the bottom of the board, drawn as seen from the top. When you flip the board to solder, left and right swap, so column 1 ends up on your right.

ATX header placement (top view, zoomed)

  1. Orientation: the long side runs front-to-back along the right edge. The latch faces the wall (right). There is about 1 cm between it and the wall, enough to press the latch when you unplug. Pins 1 and 13 are at the back. The row facing the Pico is pins 1–12.
  2. Check with the PSU cable before drilling: plug it in. The purple (pin 9) and yellow (pin 10) wires must be in the row facing the Pico, and the green (pin 16) in the row next to the wall.
  3. Drill: the pins are 4.2 mm apart and don't line up with the 2.54 mm holes. Mark through the header, drill 24 × ~1.8 mm plus the 2 locating pegs, push it in, and glue it.
  4. Wire 5 pins (coloured). GND 3, 5VSB 9 and 12V 10 come straight off the row facing the Pico. The only two from the wall-side row are PS_ON 16 and GND 24. Each gets a short link to the free column 24, then runs round the end of the header (PS_ON to the back, GND to the front). No wire passes between pins and none cross.

ATX header seen from the wall side

How to read the zoomed view

  • White square: an ATX pin you don't use. Leave it unsoldered, or solder it only to hold the header.
  • Coloured square: a pin you wire. The colour matches the PSU wire colour group (red = 5VSB purple wire, green = PS_ON, yellow = 12V, black = GND).
  • Dashed line: a short insulated wire on the bottom of the board, from that pin to its pad in column 24.
  • Round dot: the pad in column 24 where the wire lands. Solder paths continue from there.
  • Grey tab on the side: the latch. It faces right, toward the wall.
  • 1–12 / 13–24Pin rows. The row facing the Pico is 1–12 and the latch/wall-side row is 13–24, with pins 1 and 13 at the back end.

The pin holes have square and rounded shapes that match the PSU plug, so the plug only goes in one way. Plug the PSU cable in once before drilling to check the header is the right way round: the green wire should be in the latch-side row.

Pico Micro-USBBC-250 USB port—ordinary straight cable, forward over the free front rows

Pico header pins: left column (col 6), rows 5→24 = pins 21→40. Right column (col 13), rows 5→24 = pins 20→1. 5VSB (through D1) goes to pin 39 VSYS at (6,23). Pin 40 VBUS at (6,24), the corner pin beside the USB socket, must stay empty — 5 V there flows out of the USB cable into the switched-off BC-250. Also leave RUN, VREF, 3V3 and 3V3_EN unconnected. Firmware pins: GP15 PSU · GP17 button · GP16 LED · GP2 TPM.
